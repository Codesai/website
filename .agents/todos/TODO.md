## Prioritisation criteria and limitations

The two AI courses take priority because of the requested commercial focus and their median mobile LCP of 5.73 s and 5.62 s. Publications has the highest excess transfer size and variability. Images without dimensions provide direct evidence for the CLS fixes. The three P0 areas can be addressed in small, verifiable changes independently of P2 maintenance.

The reports use Lighthouse 13.4.1 with simulated network and CPU conditions. Field Core Web Vitals still need verification in CrUX or Search Console, including INP and actual p75 values. Verify the effects of analytics, fonts, and compression independently before attributing a result to them. Keep the wide mobile ranges in subsequent comparisons.
